## 🚀 Why Stellar Drift Exists

In the vast cosmos of space simulation games, there's a common friction point: the constant worry about energy reserves and hull integrity getting in the way of actual exploration. Stellar Drift was born from a simple observation – sometimes you just want to practice docking maneuvers, chart unknown asteroid fields, and perfect your slingshot trajectories without the anxiety of a dwindling power core.

This project is not about bypassing challenge; it's about **redefining what you choose to challenge yourself with**. When the life-support timers and fuel gauges stop demanding your attention, you're free to focus on the beautiful, complex choreography of orbital physics.

## 🎮 Getting Started

### System Requirements

- **Operating System:** Linux (any modern distribution) or Steam Deck (SteamOS 3.x+)
- **Processor:** 64-bit architecture, 2.0 GHz or better
- **Memory:** 4 GB RAM minimum
- **Storage:** 150 MB available space
- **Graphics:** Vulkan-capable GPU recommended

### Installation Journey

The installation process follows the rhythm of a well-orchestrated launch sequence. First, you'll want to obtain the trainer package from the official release channel. Once downloaded, you'll notice our package is designed with portability in mind – no deep system modifications required, just a simple extraction and execution path.

For Steam Deck users, we've crafted a **handheld-specific layout** that makes thumbstick control feel as natural as breathing. The desktop experience offers granular mouse-and-keyboard mapping that respects your preferred control schemes.

## 📖 How It Works

The trainer operates by sending **precise, calculated instructions** to the game's memory space, adjusting specific values that govern your character's vitality and the ship's structural integrity. This is accomplished through a sophisticated system that respects the game's architecture while providing you with the freedom you seek.

Our documentation includes a **technical whitepaper** (in the `docs/` folder) that explains the memory-mapping techniques we employ, written in accessible language so even a beginner can understand the beautiful complexity of what's happening under the hood.

## 🧠 Technical Deep Dive

The core engine of Stellar Drift is written in **Rust**, chosen for its memory safety guarantees and near-zero overhead – perfect for interacting with a live target process without introducing flakiness. The UI layer is crafted with **web technologies** (TypeScript + React) to allow for rapid theming and responsive layouts.

The communication between the UI and the core engine happens over a **WebSocket bridge** on localhost, which keeps the architecture cleanly separated and security-conscious.

### Memory Interfacing

Our engine uses **process_vm_readv/writev** system calls on Linux to communicate with the target application's memory space. This approach is stable across kernel updates and doesn't require any kernel modules or privileged access – very much in line with our philosophy of being a lightweight companion.

For Steam Deck, we've also implemented a **controller overlay** that visualizes button mappings directly on-screen, a feature that emerged from community feedback during early beta testing.
